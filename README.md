# Automation of Pharmacy Department in Medical Center

## 1. Project Overview
The Intelligent Pharmacy Automation System aims to improve pharmacy operations within medical centers through digitalization, inventory automation, and data-driven decision-making.

The proposed system will help manage medication inventory, monitor expiration dates, track dispensing activities, and improve coordination between the pharmacy, medical warehouses, doctors, and nursing staff.

## 2. Problem Statement
Many medical centers face operational challenges in pharmacy management, including:
- Manual medication registration and dispensing, which may lead to human errors.
- Difficulty tracking expiration dates and identifying medications approaching expiration.
- Unexpected shortages of essential medications or overstocking.
- Limited real-time coordination between the pharmacy, warehouses, doctors, and nursing staff.
- Difficulty analyzing medication consumption and generating accurate inventory reports.

## 3. Proposed Solution
The project proposes a centralized digital system to automate pharmacy department operations. The system will provide medication inventory tracking, expiration alerts, stock-level monitoring, dispensing records, and management reports.

Future development may incorporate data analytics and AI-based forecasting to estimate medication demand and support inventory planning.

## 4. Main Objectives
- Improve medication inventory accuracy.
- Reduce medication waste caused by expiration.
- Minimize stock shortages and unnecessary overstocking.
- Improve traceability of medication dispensing.
- Enhance coordination among relevant medical departments.
- Support management decisions through accurate reports and analytics.

## 5. Proposed Features
- Medication database and inventory management.
- Batch number and expiration date tracking.
- Low-stock and near-expiration notifications.
- Medication dispensing records.
- Inventory movement tracking.
- Consumption and inventory reports.
- Role-based access for authorized users.
- Potential AI-based demand forecasting in future versions.

## 6. Target Users
- Pharmacists and pharmacy staff.
- Medical warehouse personnel.
- Doctors and nursing staff, according to their authorized roles.
- Medical center administrators.

## 7. Implementation Plan
1. Requirements gathering and workflow analysis.
2. System design and database planning.
3. Development of core inventory and medication management features.
4. Implementation of alerts and reporting.
5. Testing, evaluation, and refinement.
6. Future integration with other medical center systems and analytics tools.

## 8. Expected Impact
The proposed system is expected to improve inventory visibility, reduce avoidable medication waste, support timely replenishment, improve operational efficiency, and provide more reliable information for pharmacy management.

## 9. Project Status
**Status:** Project Proposal

The features described in this document are proposed capabilities and should not be considered implemented or clinically validated unless confirmed through subsequent development and testing.
